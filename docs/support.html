Support

Two ways to get help: open an issue on GitHub for the fastest response, or send an email to nic@glazkov.com.

Common questions

The app asks for my location. Why?

Your location is the speed source. The app calculates your speed from GPS and compares it with the targets in your procedure. Give the app precise location access; the approximate option cannot supply a usable speed. The app uses the location for nothing else — see the privacy policy.

The screen shows "There is no GPS signal yet."

GPS needs a view of the sky. The signal usually arrives in some seconds outdoors. In a garage or a tunnel there is no signal, and the run waits until the signal comes back.

Does the run stop when the screen goes off or a call comes in?

No. The run continues. On Android, the instruction stays in the notification. On iOS, it stays in a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. The spoken cues continue in the two conditions.

Which procedure do I use for my pads?

Use the instructions from your pad manufacturer if you have them — they have priority. The presets in the app are usual community procedures, and you can edit each value in the procedure editor.

How do I install the app?

Android: download the APK from the latest release. iOS: join through TestFlight.

Feedback and defects

A clear report helps: the device, the app version (in Settings → About), what you did, and what the app did. On iOS, you can also send feedback with a screenshot directly from TestFlight.
